# Research Gap Analysis

Despite rich security literature targeting Wi-Fi routers (`SOHO` MIPS/ARM), ICS PLCs, and Android smartphones, **first-generation proprietary Linux Digital Television (`DTV`) platforms** exhibit four systematic gaps in prior public work:

| Identified Gap | Prevailing Limitation in Prior Work | Resolved in This Study (`LE32S5760 / MT5880`) | Remaining Open Gap (Requires Hardware Access) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gap 1: End-to-End Silicon-to-UI Proof Chain** | Papers often inspect either U-Boot strings *or* rootfs web binaries separately without linking MMIO OTP fuses through `dm-verity` to application daemons. | Reconstructs **all 8 boot/verify boundaries** with exact virtual addresses (`0xf0008664`, `0xfb000520`, `0xc0009594`, `0xc0028df8`, `0x404f28`, `0x036190`, `0x1889a0`, `0x1db58c`). | Physical JTAG/glitching measurement of BootROM silicon mask code. |
| **Gap 2: MediaTek `NFSB` v2 + `33B`-`\n` `.upg` Crypto Specs** | Hobbyist forum threads contain fragmented recipes or outdated keys that fail silently at `"REINICIAR OU TENTAR NOVAMENTE"`. | Provides mathematical verification scripts proving both `pow(sig,3,N) == 0^96||SHA256` across `36,281` blocks **and** the exact `sub_404f28` `"/dev/nand_10"` `-22 ENOENT` failure + `33`-byte `\n` MD5 key quirk. | Online OTA delta endpoint protocol capture (`https://www.ecdinterface.philips.com/...`). |
| **Gap 3: Quantitative Trust-Store & EoL SBOM Audits on DTVs** | Security disclosures often report single CVEs (`root` shell / RCE) without full component SBOMs (`SPDX`/`CycloneDX`) or CA bundle decay metrics. | Generates full `SPDX-2.3` (`27` packages), `CycloneDX 1.5` (`26` components), `4,004`-row filesystem CSV, and OpenSSL audit of all `186` certs in `opcacrt6.pem` (`9` expired <=`2016`, `57` <`2026`). | Live CVE exploitability fuzzing inside `qemu-system-arm` or physical UART harness. |
| **Gap 4: Non-Destructive Preservation Boundary** | Custom ROM projects frequently omit explicit cryptographic brick analysis when lacking hardware flashers. | Demonstrates why `mtd12` (`/3rd_rw`) via `<TAG>smth</TAG><METHOD>FILE</METHOD>` represents the safest upper bound of modification on locked units without chip programmers. | Physical board teardown photos (`715G7904M` PCB verification) & live UART capture. |
